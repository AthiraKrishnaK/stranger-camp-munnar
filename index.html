FAQ

Got Questions?

Everything you need to know before booking.

Is it safe for solo female travellers?
▼

Absolutely. We maintain a respectful, zero-tolerance environment. Every participant is screened, and our hosts are trained for safety and inclusion. Many solo women attend every camp.

I'm an introvert. Will I enjoy this?
▼

100% yes. Activities are structured so nobody is pushed into anything uncomfortable. Many of our best connections actually come from quieter campers.

What if it rains in Munnar?
▼

Munnar rain honestly adds to vibe. Tents are fully waterproof and we have indoor backup spaces for every activity. Severe weather is communicated 48 hours in advance.

Is alcohol allowed?
▼

No. This is a sober, mindful experience — intentionally. Real connections, no liquid courage. Violation = immediate removal, no exceptions.

What's the refund / cancellation policy?
▼

All bookings are non-refundable. However, in case of unforeseen circumstances, you may transfer your booking to another person or reschedule based on availability.

What should I bring?
▼

Warm clothes (Munnar nights hit 12–15°C!), trekking shoes, personal toiletries, a power bank, and your camera. We'll send a full packing list on WhatsApp after booking.

What age group attends?
▼

Mostly 20–35 year olds — students, working professionals, freelancers. A good mix that makes it interesting.
